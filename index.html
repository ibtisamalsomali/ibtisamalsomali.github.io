About

Ibtisam Alsomali is a Consultant at Maven Insights in Riyadh, with three years of experience serving leading public and private sector organizations across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Her work translates organizational objectives into structured, actionable frameworks, clearly defined roles and responsibilities, and reporting that supports executive decision-making.

She has designed risk assessment frameworks, risk management frameworks and strategies, and corporate performance management and KPI frameworks. She has also supported PMO governance and conducted process assessments to identify gaps and improvement opportunities.

Ibtisam holds the Governance, Risk and Compliance Professional (GRCP) certification from OCEG and the Certified Performance Management Professional credential from The KPI Institute. She earned a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from King Saud University, which underpins her strength in data analysis, dashboards and automation.
